DEMO FOR MEETING — TWO EVENTS IN ONE PROJECT

Open the app normally. The Competition events page contains both demo states:

1) DEMO • NOT STARTED • 48 NATIONS • PETANQUE SHOOTING 2026
   - 48 countries
   - 8 lanes / 6 countries per lane
   - no score entries
   - all athletes waiting
   - initial 0-point tie displays Class 1 for everyone
   - no Round 2 / Knockout results yet

2) DEMO • FINISHED • 48 NATIONS • PETANQUE SHOOTING 2026
   - Round 1 completed
   - Round 2 completed
   - Quarter Final, Semi Final and Final completed
   - neutral meeting demo (Belgium vs Spain final)

Both are inside the SAME instance/shooting.db.
Do not replace the bundled database with an older instance/shooting.db before the meeting.

If you run seed_demo_48_countries_sqlite.py, it resets ONLY the FINISHED demo event.
